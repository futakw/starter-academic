abstract: "We propose Text-Printed Image (TPI), which generates synthetic images by directly rendering textual descriptions on a plain white canvas, bridging the modality gap between text and images for cost-efficient training of large vision-language models. TPI outperforms diffusion-based synthetic images across multiple benchmarks while preserving semantic accuracy."

highlights:
- Proposes Text-Printed Image (TPI), a method that synthesizes training images by rendering text directly on a plain canvas, enabling text-centric VLM training without costly image generation.
- Bridges the image-text modality gap for large vision-language models using only textual data, dramatically reducing training cost.
- Demonstrates that TPI outperforms diffusion-based synthetic images across multiple benchmarks while better preserving semantic accuracy.
